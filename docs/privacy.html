spherecam – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

spherecam is an app for capturing 360° panoramas. It works entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. The developer does not collect, receive, store or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Camera and sensors

The app asks for the camera permission to take the photos that make up a panorama. It also reads the phone's motion sensors (gyroscope) to guide you while capturing. Photos and sensor data are saved only in the app's private storage on your device. The panorama is stitched on your device, without any network access.

Finished panoramas

Each stitched panorama is also saved to your phone's photo gallery (Pictures/SphereCam), where your gallery app can show it as a 360° photo. What happens to it there, for example a cloud backup, depends on your gallery app and its settings.

Exporting

If you use "Export (ZIP)" or "Share panorama", the app hands the file to the app you pick in the Android share dialog. From that point the file is handled by that app under its own privacy policy. The app itself never sends anything anywhere.

Deleting your data

Delete a session in the app or uninstall the app, and all its data is removed from your device. Panoramas saved to the photo gallery stay there until you delete them in your gallery app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
